# Template provenance

Visual starting point: [w-r-s/academic-homepage-template](https://github.com/w-r-s/academic-homepage-template), commit `3e688b9cdb8928aa90ce2469b932cd4661723f97`.

The portrait introduction, narrow academic page, warm accent, and illustrated publication rows are retained as the design basis. The markup and stylesheet have been refactored for semantic headings, small screens, two static languages, accessible figure enlargement, and data-driven content. Sample-author replacement, link-reset scripts, sample projects, custom cursors, and the rotating tag cloud are omitted.

Personal portrait: `me.jpg`, provided by Qiming Zhu. OS-native architecture figure: supplied by Qiming Zhu. TierMem overview: official repository `FreedomIntelligence/Tiermem/frame.jpg`. MTalk-Bench overview: Figure 1 of arXiv:2508.18240v2.

No license is inferred for the upstream repository or third-party research figures. Attribution and source links are preserved.
