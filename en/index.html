PERSONALIZED AGENTS

Qiming Zhu朱淇铭

I am a research master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Benyou Wang.

I aim to advance Personalized Agents: working agents and mobile agents that understand individuals and collaborate with them over time, across work and everyday life.

Understanding you better. I study long-term memory and explore RSI, with the goal of helping agents learn from interaction history and feedback, understand personal preferences and needs, and improve how they collaborate.

Handling and managing tasks better. Through OS-native support, I explore agents that track goals and coordinate tasks over time and across applications as user needs and environments evolve. Multi-turn interaction evaluation helps me examine whether these capabilities truly serve the user.

Let’s explore and build together, taking steps toward the JARVIS we envision.

COLM 2026First author

TierMem: Balancing Compressed Memory and Raw Evidence for Long-Horizon Agent Memory

Qiming Zhu, Shunian Chen, Rui Yu, Zhehao Wu, Benyou Wang

A two-tier agent memory framework that uses compact summaries by default and recovers raw evidence when more detail is needed. Provenance links and verified write-back help balance answer quality, context cost, and latency over long interactions.

MTalk-Bench: Evaluating Speech-to-Speech Models in Multi-Turn Dialogues via Arena-style and Rubrics Protocols

Yuhao Du, Qianwei Huang, Guo Zhu, Zhanchen Dai, Shunian Chen, Qiming Zhu, Le Pan, Minghao Chen, Yuhao Zhang, Li Zhou, Benyou Wang, Haizhou Li

A multi-turn speech-to-speech benchmark covering semantic content, paralinguistic cues, and ambient sounds. It combines Arena-style comparisons with hierarchical rubrics to diagnose model capabilities and assess the reliability of human and LLM judges.
